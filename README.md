# Sodalis

Sodalis is an AI-powered family care coordination app that helps isolated older adults stay connected with the people who matter most. The platform combines outbound voice check-ins, AI-powered summarization, a family dashboard, opportunity matching, and push notifications to turn a simple check-in into a real support loop.

It is designed for a demo-friendly v1 setup where:
- a senior receives an outbound voice check-in
- an AI assistant reads the transcript and extracts wellbeing signals and relationship opportunities
- a family dashboard surfaces alerts and suggested contact opportunities
- a scheduler recommends visit or call slots
- family members receive timely web push notifications and can confirm interest

---

## Why this exists

Many older adults feel disconnected and may not have regular contact with family. Sodalis helps bridge that gap by scheduling and surfacing moments of connection in a way that is low-friction and respectful for the senior.

Instead of requiring the elder to navigate an app, the system reaches them by phone and summarizes what happened afterward, then helps the family act on the right opportunities.

---

## Core features

- Outbound voice check-ins via Vapi
- AI extraction of:
  - mood and wellbeing
  - health or safety concerns
  - relationship opportunities
  - contact requests and family follow-ups
- Family-facing dashboard showing check-ins, alerts, and opportunities
- Scheduler for matching family availability to recommended call or visit windows
- Push notifications to family members through Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Support for importing availability from Google Calendar
- Internal "who are you?" member selection flow for demo scenarios
- Warm, human-friendly outreach messaging generated by AI

## Architecture overview

This project is structured as a full-stack app with a family dashboard, voice-driven check-in flow, AI decision layer, and notification pipeline:

- Frontend: Next.js app with dashboard and member flows
- Backend/API: app routes under the Next.js app router
- Database: Supabase for core data, member profiles, availability, opportunities, alerts, and check-ins
- Voice: Vapi outbound calls and webhook processing
- AI: Gemini for extracting wellbeing data, suggesting opportunities, and coordinating contact actions
- Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging for browser push notifications
